01 /Data We Collect

When you visit BEYE Store, we may automatically collect limited technical data to help us understand how the site is used:

  • ip — anonymized address and approximate geographic location
  • device — browser type, device model, and operating system
  • behavior — pages visited, time spent, and navigation patterns
  • referrer — traffic source: search, social media, or direct

We do not collect your name, email, or personal details unless you voluntarily contact us.

02 /How We Use It

The data we collect is used exclusively for:

  • Improving the store's content and user experience
  • Understanding which apps and categories are most popular
  • Responding to messages and developer inquiries

We will never sell your data or share it with third parties beyond what is required to operate this site.

03 /Third-Party Services

BEYE Store may use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics — visitor statistics and behavior analytics
  • Netlify — hosting platform that may log access requests
  • Firebase / AdMob — used in distributed apps for analytics and ads

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these services independently.

04 /Advertising

Apps distributed through BEYE Store may use Google AdMob or similar advertising SDKs. These services may display personalized ads based on your interests and usage behavior.

Each individual app has its own ad configuration. You can opt out of personalized ads through your device settings under Google → Ads.

05 /Children's Privacy

BEYE Store is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

If you believe a child has submitted data without parental consent, please contact us immediately and we will delete it promptly.
